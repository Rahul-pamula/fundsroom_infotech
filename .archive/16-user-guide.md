# 16. User Guide

## Navigating the Platform

### Sales Executives
- Use **CRM** to add prospective customers and change their status to ACTIVE.
- Use the **Customer Details** page to log phone call notes and schedule follow-ups.
- Head to **Challans** to draft a new sales order for an ACTIVE customer, adding items from the catalog.

### Warehouse Managers
- Check the **Dashboard** daily for low-stock badges.
- Use **Inventory -> Adjust Stock** to register newly arrived purchase orders (IN) or damage write-offs (OUT).
- Monitor the **Challans** page. When Sales notifies you a draft is ready, click **Confirm Challan** to officially dispatch and deduct inventory.

### Administrators
- You have complete global access.
- Use **Products** to expand the master catalog, assigning categories, SKUs, and unit prices.

### Account Executives
- Your role is purely auditing.
- View **Stock Movements** to ensure inventory isn't missing.
- View **Challans** to process billing against confirmed dispatches.
