# 2. Problem Statement

Small-to-medium wholesale and distribution businesses often struggle with fragmented operational processes. The Mini ERP + CRM Operations Portal was built to solve specific organizational issues:

### Customer Information Management
Sales teams lack a centralized directory for leads and active customers. Information is often siloed, making it difficult to hand over accounts or track historical context.

### Follow-up Management
Without a structured system, sales representatives lose track of when to contact prospects, resulting in dropped leads and lost revenue.

### Product Catalog Management
Maintaining a master product list across different departments often leads to pricing discrepancies and SKU mismatches. 

### Inventory Visibility
Warehouse and sales teams lack real-time visibility into available stock, often resulting in sales representatives promising inventory that does not exist.

### Stock Movement Tracking
Modifying stock without an immutable ledger creates accountability issues. It becomes impossible to audit whether stock was lost, damaged, sold, or manually adjusted.

### Sales Challan Management
Fulfillment involves creating dispatch documents (Challans). Without digital transaction locks, multiple salespeople might simultaneously confirm challans for the same low-stock item, creating negative inventory balances.

### Role-Based Access
Accounts should not modify inventory, and sales should not create product catalogs. Lacking Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) causes internal data corruption.

### Operational Visibility
Executives require a high-level summary of active leads, low stock, and daily shipments to make informed business decisions.
